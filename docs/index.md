## 75Way Technologies, Mohali

A four-month industrial internship structured in two parts.

!!! info "Multi-stack Training (Weeks 1–8)"
    Instructor-led training covering Python with Django and FastAPI, the MERN stack, Flutter, and React Native. Concluded with an evaluation test in February that determined project assignment.

!!! success "Manage Business Platform (Weeks 9–16)"
    Production-grade centralized authentication, user management, access control, activity logging, and subscription management platform built for a client of 75Way. Worked in a three-member team using Agile methodology with primary ownership of the Subscription and Access Management modules.

[Go to 75Way Overview →](phase-1-75way/overview.md)

## GNDEC TnP Cell, Ludhiana

A six-week on-campus training stint at the Training and Placement Cell of Guru Nanak Dev Engineering College, delivering two distinct software systems for the institute.

!!! note "Training Letter Management Plugin (Weeks 17–18)"
    Custom Moodle local plugin that digitizes the industrial training letter application, approval, and verification workflow, integrated into the GNDEC Moodle LMS.

!!! tip "Training and Placement Portal (Weeks 19–22)"
    Full-stack web application that automates the complete campus placement lifecycle, replacing the existing manual Google Forms-based workflow with a role-based, database-driven system.
